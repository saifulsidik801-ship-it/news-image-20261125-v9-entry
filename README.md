One image summing up the world's news of **Wednesday 25 November 2026**:
**31 stories** — 3 heroes (US tariffs on 14 economies · Kospi −6.2% chip
sell-off · Shenzhou-24 flies Pakistan's first astronaut to Tiangong) +
10 mids with full scene illustrations + 18 more in the list. Every story
happened or was first reported on 25 November 2026; the DATE CHECK box
names only stories that are *not* on the page, plus two not-yet items
(US Thanksgiving, Argentina 2027 budget vote).

## Files

- `entry.png` — the entry image (1024×1024 PNG, ≤1 MiB).
- `render.py` — draws `entry.png` from scratch with PIL (flat vector
  illustration drawn in code, no photos). Run: `python3 render.py`.
- `main.py` — the entrypoint (standard library only): copies `entry.png`
  to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png`. Run:
  `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py`.

## Notes

- Tariff rates are the USTR Section 301 proposal of 25 Nov 2026:
  China 20%, India 25% (17% if a deal is struck), Vietnam 35%
  (20% if a deal is struck), EU 15%.
- Cairo tanker-fire toll is 31 dead (Reuters early figure 21, later
  BBC/AFP reports 31).
- Zaporizhzhia is shown on the power-loss angle only (17th loss this
  year, power out in five regions) — no death toll printed.
- Every drawing is original, drawn in code, not photos.
